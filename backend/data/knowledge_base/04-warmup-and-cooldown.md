# Warm-up and Cool-down

## Warm-up
A warm-up of about 5 to 10 minutes prepares the body for exercise. Start with light cardio such as brisk walking, cycling or jumping jacks to raise heart rate and body temperature, then do dynamic movements such as arm circles, leg swings, hip circles, bodyweight squats and walking lunges. Finish with one or two lighter practice sets of the first exercise.

## Cool-down
A cool-down of about 5 minutes lets heart rate come down gradually. Walk slowly, then do gentle static stretches of the muscles you trained, holding each stretch for about 20 to 30 seconds without bouncing and without sharp pain.

## Stretching notes
Dynamic movement is generally preferred before training, while static stretching is more useful after training or as separate flexibility work.
