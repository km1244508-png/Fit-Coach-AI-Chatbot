# Push-up and Plank Form

## Push-up setup
Place the hands slightly wider than shoulder-width under the shoulders, with the body in a straight line from head to heels. Squeeze the glutes and brace the abdominals so the hips do not sag or pike.

## Push-up movement
Lower the chest toward the floor with the elbows angled about 30 to 45 degrees from the body rather than flared straight out at 90 degrees. Press the floor away to return to the start. Keep the neck neutral by looking slightly ahead of the hands.

## Push-up progressions
If a full push-up is too hard, start with incline push-ups with hands on a bench or counter, or knee push-ups, and gradually reduce the incline as you get stronger.

## Plank
For a forearm plank, place the elbows under the shoulders and keep the body in a straight line. Do not let the lower back sag. Start with sets of 15 to 30 seconds and build up gradually. Quality matters more than duration, so stop the set when form breaks down.

## Wrist and shoulder discomfort
Use push-up handles or fists, or choose incline push-ups, if the wrists feel uncomfortable. Stop if there is sharp shoulder pain.
