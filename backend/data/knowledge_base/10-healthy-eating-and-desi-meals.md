# Healthy Eating Patterns and Desi Meal Ideas

## Balanced plate
A practical plate contains about half vegetables or salad, about a quarter protein (dal, chicken, fish, eggs, paneer, chickpeas, tofu) and about a quarter whole-grain or starchy carbohydrate (roti, brown rice, oats). Add a small amount of healthy fat such as nuts, seeds or olive or mustard oil.

## Fiber
Dietary fiber supports digestion, fullness and heart health. Common adult targets are about 25 grams per day for women and 38 grams per day for men. Good sources are whole grains, dal, beans, chickpeas, vegetables, fruits, nuts and seeds. Increase fiber gradually and drink enough water.

## Breakfast ideas
Vegetable omelette with a whole-wheat roti, oats with milk and fruit, besan chilla with yogurt, or Greek-style yogurt with nuts and fruit.

## Lunch and dinner ideas
Dal with a small portion of rice or roti and a large salad, grilled or baked chicken or fish with sauteed vegetables, chana chaat with cucumber and tomato, or vegetable curry with paneer and one roti.

## Snack ideas
Roasted chickpeas, a handful of unsalted nuts, fruit with yogurt, boiled eggs, or sprouts salad.

## Practical swaps
Choose grilling, baking or air-frying over deep frying. Choose water, unsweetened tea or lassi without sugar instead of sugary soft drinks and sweetened juices. Use smaller portions of fried snacks and sweets, and enjoy them occasionally without guilt.

## Special diets
People with diabetes, kidney disease, food allergies, pregnancy, or other medical needs should follow the advice of their doctor or a registered dietitian.
