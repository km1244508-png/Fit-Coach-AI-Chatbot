# Cardio and Weekly Activity Guidelines

## General adult recommendations
The World Health Organization recommends that adults aged 18 to 64 do at least 150 to 300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week, or at least 75 to 150 minutes of vigorous-intensity aerobic activity per week, or an equivalent combination. Adults should also do muscle-strengthening activities at moderate or greater intensity on 2 or more days per week.

## Intensity
Moderate intensity means you can talk but not sing, such as brisk walking or easy cycling. Vigorous intensity means you can say only a few words without pausing for breath, such as running or fast cycling.

## Starting from zero
Beginners can start with 10 to 15 minute brisk walks and build gradually, adding about 5 minutes per session each week. Any activity is better than none, and breaking activity into short bouts throughout the day counts.

## Daily movement
Reduce long periods of sitting by standing or walking for a few minutes every hour.

## Cardio and fat loss
Cardio helps increase energy expenditure and supports heart health, but weight loss still depends mostly on overall calorie balance. Combining cardio with strength training helps keep muscle while losing fat.

## Medical clearance
People with heart disease, uncontrolled blood pressure, diabetes, pregnancy, or new chest discomfort should talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program.
